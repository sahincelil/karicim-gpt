# SYSTEM ARCHIVE — Visual Identity

Version: 1.0
Status: Initial creative system for a four-week pilot
Channel: SYSTEM ARCHIVE // HUMAN PROTOCOL
Language: Turkish

## 1. Brand idea

**A dark science archive that turns perception into an experiment.**

SYSTEM ARCHIVE should feel like a field notebook from a modern perception lab: curious, precise, cinematic, and a little uncanny. The visual system must make a scientific question feel immediate without presenting speculation as fact.

## 2. Core palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Archive Black | #090B10 | Main background |
| Slate Panel | #121722 | Cards, labels, secondary surfaces |
| Signal Cyan | #73E5F4 | Measurement lines, key scientific marks, links |
| Reveal Amber | #FFB84A | The moment of discovery, emphasis |
| Archive White | #F2F4F7 | Main text |
| Alert Red | #FF4D5A | Rare warning / contradiction accent only |

Use black and white as the base. Cyan and amber should each have a clear job. Alert Red is not a general decoration color.

## 3. Typography

Suggested starting pair, subject to availability in the Canva workspace:
- **Headlines:** Montserrat ExtraBold or a similarly condensed, heavy sans serif.
- **Body / labels:** Inter or a neutral sans serif with strong small-size legibility.
- Use uppercase for short thumbnail headlines; sentence case for descriptions and explanatory text.
- Keep fine print out of thumbnails. Never use a decorative font for scientific explanations.

## 4. Logo direction

Use a compact **SA / archive-index monogram** paired with the wordmark SYSTEM ARCHIVE. The mark should be geometric and readable at small sizes:
- A square or corner-bracket frame suggests an indexed archive.
- One cyan measurement notch or scan marker provides recognition.
- Wordmark in white; optional cyan divider.
- Avoid skulls, blood motifs, generic brains, robot heads, and clichéd atom icons.

This is a direction for the first logo exploration, not a claim that a final logo has already been approved.

## 5. Thumbnail system

**Canvas:** 16:9 YouTube thumbnail.

Composition:
1. One dominant optical illusion, object, eye, geometric shape, or experiment.
2. One headline of 2–4 Turkish words, ideally readable at phone size.
3. Small SYSTEM ARCHIVE stamp in a consistent corner.
4. A restrained grid, crosshair, ruler, index number, or measurement annotation.
5. Strong separation between subject and background; preserve negative space.

Headline examples:
- GÖZÜN YANILIYOR
- HANGİSİ DAHA UZUN?
- BEYNİN BOŞLUĞU DOLDURUYOR

Rules:
- One visual question per thumbnail.
- Do not place the entire video title on the image.
- Do not add fake data, fake labels, or scientific-looking numbers that imply evidence.
- Do not use a visual illusion that contradicts the actual experiment shown in the video.
- Check legibility at small mobile preview size before approval.

## 6. Motion and on-screen graphics

- Use thin cyan lines for measurements, pointers, and framing.
- Use amber only when the reveal or explanation begins.
- Keep transitions purposeful: cut, brief mask, or measured zoom; avoid constant glitch effects.
- Captions should be high contrast, concise, and synchronized. Do not cover the key illusion.
- Keep sound effects below narration and do not use them to disguise unclear information.

## 7. Voice and editorial tone

**Curious, direct, evidence-aware.**
- Ask a question before explaining.
- Invite the viewer to test what they see.
- Explain the mechanism in ordinary Turkish.
- Distinguish established findings from hypotheses and open questions.
- Avoid sensational claims such as “your brain is broken” unless clearly framed as a metaphor.

## 8. Reusable asset naming

Use:
`SA_[FORMAT]_[TOPIC]_[YYYYMMDD]_v01`

Examples:
- `SA_THUMB_ILLUSION_20261114_v01`
- `SA_SHORTS_PERCEPTION_20261114_v01`
- `SA_OVERLAY_CYAN_MEASURE_v01`

## 9. Approval checklist

- [ ] One clear focal point
- [ ] Headline readable on a phone
- [ ] Palette used consistently
- [ ] SYSTEM ARCHIVE mark visible but not dominant
- [ ] No misleading scientific visual or fabricated annotation
- [ ] Captions and important details remain inside safe margins
- [ ] Final export checked at actual YouTube thumbnail size

## 10. Pilot review

Use this system for four weeks. Review thumbnail clarity, channel recognition, click-through / viewed-vs-swiped signals, and whether the visuals faithfully represent the video. Adjust from comparable cohorts, not from one isolated upload.
