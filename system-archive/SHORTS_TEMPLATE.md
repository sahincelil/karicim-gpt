# Shorts script template

Target duration: 20–35 seconds. Adjust to the idea; do not pad to hit a number.

## 1. Hook (0–2s)
One concrete question or visual contradiction. Avoid generic openings.

## 2. Experiment (2–8s)
Show the illusion, comparison, or quick test. Keep it readable on a phone and give viewers time to predict.

## 3. Reveal (8–15s)
Deliver the surprising result. It must directly answer the opening question.

## 4. Explanation (15–27s)
Explain one mechanism in plain Turkish. Use one scientific idea, not a pile of jargon. Qualify uncertain claims.

## 5. Close (last 2–4s)
Leave one memorable question or a concise invitation to follow the series.

## Production checklist
- [ ] First frame communicates the subject without sound.
- [ ] Spoken hook and on-screen text agree.
- [ ] Captions are readable and correctly punctuated.
- [ ] Visual demonstrates the claim.
- [ ] Explanation is scientifically defensible.
- [ ] Sound is licensed, original, or cleared.
- [ ] Description includes sources for factual claims.
- [ ] No unsupported medical or psychological promises.
