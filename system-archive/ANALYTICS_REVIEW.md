## Interpretation
### Appeal
What made viewers choose the video? Check topic, title, first frame, thumbnail, and opening line.

### Engagement
Where did viewers stay, replay, or leave? Identify the first meaningful drop-off and whether the payoff arrived too late.

### Satisfaction
Look for returning viewers, subscribers gained, meaningful comments, and other available satisfaction signals.

## Data hygiene
- Do not compare Shorts directly with long-form.
- Note upload age and exposure differences.
- Record unavailable metrics as “not available”, never zero.
- Check metric definition changes before comparing periods.
- Treat small samples as directional, not conclusive.
