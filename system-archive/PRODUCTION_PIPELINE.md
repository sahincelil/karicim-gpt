# SYSTEM ARCHIVE — Content Production Pipeline

Version: 1.0
Status: Initial operating procedure for a four-week pilot
Language: Turkish

## 1. Operating cadence

Initial test cadence:
- 3 Shorts per week
- 1 long-form video every two weeks
- 1 Community post per week

This is an experiment, not a permanent commitment. Review workload and audience response after four weeks.

## 2. Production flow

### Stage 1 — Idea intake

Every idea gets a short card with:
- Viewer question: what should the viewer want to find out?
- Visual proof: can the idea be demonstrated on screen?
- Evidence path: is there a reliable source for the central claim?
- Format: Short, long-form, or Community post.
- Intended audience payoff: what will the viewer understand or notice afterwards?

Prioritize ideas that are intriguing, demonstrable, sourceable, and explainable in one clear sentence.

### Stage 2 — Research and claim check

For each factual claim, record:
- Exact claim in plain language
- Source title, author/institution, publication date, and URL or DOI
- Whether the source is primary research, a review, or an authoritative institution
- Limitations, sample/context, and what the source does not prove

Prefer primary research and reputable scientific institutions. Use reviews to understand consensus. Do not turn correlation into causation, a narrow experiment into a universal rule, or an early hypothesis into settled fact. If a claim cannot be verified, remove it or label uncertainty.

### Stage 3 — Script

Shorts target: approximately 25–35 seconds, adjusted to the idea.

Suggested beat sheet:
1. **0–2s — Hook:** a visual contradiction or one sharp question.
2. **2–8s — Test:** show what the viewer should look at or predict.
3. **8–18s — Reveal:** expose the surprising result.
4. **18–28s — Explanation:** explain the mechanism in plain Turkish.
5. **28–35s — Close:** one takeaway or a question that invites a meaningful comment.

The hook must match the actual payoff. Do not delay the answer just to inflate retention.

### Stage 4 — Storyboard and asset plan

For every beat, note:
- What appears on screen
- Camera / motion / crop
- Caption text
- Sound or narration
- Required source image, graphic, or footage
- Whether the asset is original, licensed, or otherwise permitted

Use the SYSTEM ARCHIVE visual identity. Build around one dominant visual idea.

### Stage 5 — Edit

- Make the first frame understandable without sound.
- Keep captions short, high-contrast, and away from the experiment's critical area.
- Keep narration clear and effects subordinate.
- Use cuts and motion only when they improve understanding.
- Do not use a visual effect that changes the scientific result or makes the demonstration misleading.
- Check mobile readability and safe margins.

### Stage 6 — Quality control

A video is ready for owner review only when all checks pass:

**Scientific**
- [ ] Every factual claim has a source or is clearly framed as interpretation
- [ ] The demonstration matches the explanation
- [ ] Uncertainty and limitations are not hidden
- [ ] No unsupported medical or psychological promise

**Editorial**
- [ ] Hook, test, reveal, explanation, and close form one coherent arc
- [ ] Title and thumbnail promise the actual content
- [ ] Turkish wording is clear and natural

**Technical**
- [ ] Voice is understandable and not clipped
- [ ] Captions are synchronized and readable on mobile
- [ ] No accidental black frames, broken assets, or unsafe margins
- [ ] Music, footage, and images have appropriate usage rights

### Stage 7 — Metadata and publication

Prepare, but do not auto-publish:
- Accurate title
- Short description
- Sources and further reading
- Relevant playlist
- Thumbnail
- Captions
- Optional pinned question that follows naturally from the video

The channel owner reviews the final package and performs or explicitly approves publication. No automatic upload or channel changes are part of this SOP.

### Stage 8 — Measurement and learning

Review the same upload at:
- 24 hours: early distribution and obvious packaging issues
- 72 hours: initial retention and audience response
- 7 days: more stable cohort comparison

Record:
- Date range and data coverage
- Format and upload age
- Shorts: engaged views, viewed vs swiped away / stayed-to-watch, average view duration, average percentage viewed, subscribers gained
- Long-form: impressions, CTR, average view duration, retention, subscribers gained
- Meaningful comments and recurring viewer questions

Compare like with like: Shorts against Shorts, long-form against long-form, and similar upload-age windows. Missing data is not zero. Change one major variable per experiment where practical, such as hook framing or thumbnail subject, so the result remains interpretable.

## 3. Weekly operating rhythm

| Day | Work block |
|---|---|
| Monday | Review last week's results; choose three Short ideas |
| Tuesday | Research and source-check all three |
| Wednesday | Script and storyboard |
| Thursday | Produce / edit Short 1 and 2 |
| Friday | Produce / edit Short 3; prepare Community post |
| Weekend | Publish according to the channel schedule; capture early signals |
| Every second week | Add one long-form research and production block |

Move blocks as needed; quality and sustainable workload take precedence over the calendar.

## 4. Status labels

Use the same labels in Notion or the working board:
- IDEA
- RESEARCH
- SCRIPT
- STORYBOARD
- EDITING
- QC
- READY FOR OWNER REVIEW
- APPROVED
- PUBLISHED
- LEARNING

Only the channel owner can mark a draft APPROVED for publication.

## 5. File naming and versioning

Use:
`SA_[FORMAT]_[TOPIC]_[YYYYMMDD]_v01`

Keep source notes and project files linked to the matching video card. Never commit passwords, OAuth tokens, API keys, MFA codes, recovery codes, or private account exports.

## 6. Definition of done

A production item is complete when:
- Its central claim is source-checked
- Script and visuals tell the same story
- Scientific, editorial, and technical QC pass
- Title, thumbnail, captions, and description are ready
- The owner has reviewed the final publication package
- Results are captured at the agreed measurement windows
