## Science note

The classic color-word Stroop task measures interference when the printed color word conflicts with the ink color. The original 1935 paper reports that conflicting word stimuli increased the time required to name ink colors compared with color patches. This pack therefore says that conflicting word meaning **can slow color naming**. It does not claim that reading is completely automatic for every person or that all viewers will have the same reaction.

Primary source:
- Stroop, J. R. (1935). "Studies of interference in serial verbal reactions." *Journal of Experimental Psychology*, 18(6), 643–662. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0054651
- APA record: https://doi.org/10.1037/H0054651

Context / methodological nuance:
- MacLeod, C. M. (1991). "Half a century of research on the Stroop effect: an integrative review." *Psychological Bulletin*, 109(2), 163–203. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.109.2.163
- Stroop effects vary with task design, stimuli, congruency, experience and response method. Avoid claiming a universal fixed response time or identical effect for every viewer.

## Short A — The challenge

**Working title:** Kelimeyi Okuma, Rengini Söyle!  
**Target:** 25–32 seconds  
**Purpose:** Participation and first exposure.

### Voice script
**0–2 s:** "Kelimeyi okuma. Sadece yazının rengini söyle!"  
**2–7 s:** "MAVİ. KIRMIZI. SARI. Hazır mısın? Renkleri söyle!"  
**7–12 s:** "Bir an duraksadıysan, yalnız değilsin."  
**12–23 s:** "Kelimenin anlamı ile mürekkebin rengi çakıştığında, kelimeyi görmezden gelmek zorlaşabilir. Renk adını söylemen yavaşlayabilir."  
**23–30 s:** "Bu etkiye Stroop etkisi deniyor. Kaçını doğru yaptın?"

### Visual direction
- First frame already contains one incongruent word.
- Use large, high-contrast type. The ink color, not the word, is the answer.
- Keep the first round to three items; give enough time for a real response.
- Show the answer colors clearly after the pause.
- Avoid red/green-only distinction; use text labels and accessible contrast.

## Short B — The explanation

**Working title:** Kelimeyi Görmezden Gelmek Neden Zor?  
**Target:** 25–35 seconds  
**Purpose:** Explain the result without oversimplifying.

### Voice script
**0–3 s:** "Az önce senden rengi söylemeni istedim. Peki neden kelime araya girdi?"  
**3–11 s:** "Çünkü aynı anda iki bilgiyle karşılaştın: kelimenin anlamı ve mürekkebin rengi."  
**11–22 s:** "Bu iki bilgi çatışınca, yalnızca rengi adlandırmak daha fazla zaman alabilir. Araştırmacılar buna Stroop girişimi diyor."  
**22–31 s:** "Yani mesele dikkatsiz olman değil. Görev, çakışan bilgileri aynı anda işlemeni istiyor."

### Visual direction
- Split the screen into WORD and INK COLOR.
- Animate both labels arriving together, then briefly highlight the conflict.
- Use "daha fazla zaman alabilir"; avoid declaring that every participant is equally affected.
- Keep the visual explanation to one mechanism.

- Title A: Kelimeyi Görmezden Gelmek Neden Zor?
- Title B: Renk Testinde Beynin Neden Yavaşlıyor?
- Thumbnail words: "İKİ BİLGİ, TEK CEVAP"

- Pinned question: "Sence en zor kısım kelimeyi bastırmak mı, hızlı cevap vermek mi?"

## Long-form — Why the word gets in the way

**Working title:** Stroop Etkisi: Beynin Aynı Anda İki Bilgiyle Mücadelesi  
**Target:** 6–8 minutes  
**Purpose:** Give context, demonstration and limits.

### Structure
1. **0:00–0:20 — Cold open:** rapid color-word challenge; no logo intro.
2. **0:20–1:00 — The question:** why can naming ink color take longer when the printed word conflicts?
3. **1:00–2:00 — The original task:** explain the basic comparison between congruent/incongruent color words and neutral color patches.
4. **2:00–3:30 — What the effect shows:** conflicting information can interfere with color naming; show the response-time comparison conceptually, without inventing numeric results.
5. **3:30–5:00 — What it does not show:** not an IQ test, medical test, or proof that all reading is fully automatic in every context.
6. **5:00–6:30 — Why task design matters:** language familiarity, stimulus choices, timing, response method and practice can change results.
7. **6:30–7:30 — Takeaway:** one controlled task reveals one measurable form of interference, not the entirety of attention or intelligence.

### Required visuals
- Original-style color-word stimuli recreated from scratch.
- Neutral color-patch comparison.
- "Word meaning" and "ink color" labels.
- A conceptual timing bar labeled "illustrative, not participant data".
- Source card with the 1935 paper and DOI.

Do not display fabricated participant counts, reaction-time values or an invented replication. Any numerical reproduction must be copied from a source and labeled with its exact experiment and conditions.

## Production notes
- Voice: calm, curious, precise Turkish. Avoid sensational "brain malfunction" language.
- Sound: subtle ticking only during the challenge; duck under speech.

- Edit: show the task immediately; no logo animation before the hook.
