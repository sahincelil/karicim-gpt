Rules:
- Prefer original peer-reviewed studies for the phenomenon and current reviews for context.
- Open the source and verify title, authors, year, DOI and what the study actually measured.
- Do not present a proposed explanation as settled consensus.
- Keep a source note in the description for central scientific claims.
- Do not use a single small study to make universal claims about all viewers.
