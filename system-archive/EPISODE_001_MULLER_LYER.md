# Episode 001 — Müller-Lyer Illusion

Status: Draft visual master v2 created; narration and final editorial QC remain open.
Working title: Beynin Gözlerine Söylediği Yalan
Format: YouTube Shorts, vertical 9:16
Target duration: 30 seconds

## Viewer promise

Two line shafts can be physically equal while their surrounding arrowheads change how long they appear.

## Sequence

- 0–5s: Ask which line looks longer; show the two arrow configurations.
- 5–10s: Let the viewer register the perceptual conflict.
- 10–17s: Remove the arrowheads and show matching endpoints and equal shafts.
- 17–24s: Name the Müller-Lyer illusion and explain the contextual influence on perceived length.
- 24–30s: Close with the channel's core question: what you see is not always what you measure.

## Scientific guardrails

- The shafts must be exactly equal in the demonstration.
- Explain that arrowhead orientation/context affects perceived length.
- Do not claim that the illusion proves the eye is broken or that all viewers perceive it identically.
- Add a credible source to the final YouTube description before publication.
