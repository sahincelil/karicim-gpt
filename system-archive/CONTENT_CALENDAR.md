# SYSTEM ARCHIVE | Four-week pilot calendar

This is a flexible backlog, not a promise to publish on fixed dates.

| Slot | Format | Working title | Hook / experiment | Core explanation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Short | Beynin Gözlerine Söylediği Yalan | “İki çizgiye bak. Sence hangisi daha uzun?” | Context changes visual judgment. |
| 2 | Short | Hareketi Kesince Dünya Neden Tuhaflaşır? | A short motion / stillness demonstration | Motion adaptation and visual processing. |
| 3 | Short | Beynin Eksik Bilgiyi Nasıl Tamamlıyor? | Show a partially obscured image | Perceptual inference and context. |
| 4 | Long | Gözümüz Gerçekliği Nasıl Kuruyor? | Three simple perception experiments | Visual system, prediction, attention, interpretation. |
| 5 | Short | Zaman Neden Bazen Yavaşlar? | Compare a boring wait with a novel event | Separate time perception from clock time. |
| 6 | Short | Hafızan Bir Video Kaydı Değil | Ask viewers to recall a familiar detail | Reconstructive memory and confidence vs accuracy. |
| 7 | Short | Dikkatin Görmediğin Şeyleri Seçiyor | A selective-attention demo | Attention limits and change blindness. |
| 8 | Long | Beyin, Hafıza ve Yanlış Hatırlama | A set of memory demonstrations | Encoding, retrieval, reconstruction, uncertainty. |
| 9 | Short | Aynı Görüntü, İki Farklı Yorum | Ambiguous image | Top-down and bottom-up processing. |
| 10 | Short | Bir Saniye Neden Uzun Hissedilir? | Anticipation / surprise contrast | Attention, arousal, retrospective duration. |
| 11 | Short | Göz Yanılsaması mı, Beyin Hatası mı? | Ask viewers to predict before reveal | Perception as inference. |
| 12 | Short | Gerçekliği Ne Kadar Doğrudan Görüyoruz? | A fast recap experiment | Sensory input vs interpretation. |

## Weekly review
- Compare Shorts with Shorts and long-form with long-form.
- Prefer videos with similar age and distribution exposure.
- Note the first 1–3 seconds, payoff timing, and whether the explanation matched the hook.
- Choose one variable to test next week, not five at once.
