# SYSTEM ARCHIVE — Evolution Standard

Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Applies to: Every research, script, visual, video, publication package, and performance review.

## Operating principle

SYSTEM ARCHIVE is a versioned production system, not a sequence of disposable one-off outputs. Each iteration preserves the previous approved artifact, records what changed, and improves at least one measurable quality dimension without silently degrading another.

## Non-negotiable quality gates

1. **Scientific integrity**
   - Separate established findings, plausible interpretations, and creative framing.
   - Verify factual claims against primary research or reputable scientific institutions.
   - Never turn correlation into causation or imply that an illusion proves the brain is defective.
   - For the Müller-Lyer illusion, state that the line shafts are equal and that arrowhead context changes perceived length; avoid unsupported claims about a universal fixed perception delay.

2. **Viewer experience**
   - Establish a clear visual question in the first 1–2 seconds.
   - Show the phenomenon before explaining it.
   - Deliver a visible proof or reveal, not merely a verbal assertion.
   - Use concise Turkish captions readable on a phone.
   - End with one memorable takeaway and a consistent channel signature.

3. **Visual craft**
   - Use the SYSTEM ARCHIVE palette: Archive Black #090B10, Slate Panel #121722, Signal Cyan #73E5F4, Reveal Amber #FFB84A, Archive White #F2F4F7; Alert Red #FF4D5A sparingly.
   - Keep geometry accurate. For optical illusions, the compared line segments must be exactly equal when the concept requires equality.
   - Maintain safe margins for YouTube Shorts interface overlays.
   - Prefer purposeful motion, restrained transitions, and legible contrast over decorative effects.

4. **Audio and accessibility**
   - Treat narration, music, and sound effects as separate mix layers.
   - Keep speech intelligible above music.
   - Provide accurate Turkish captions or on-screen text for key information.
   - Never imitate a real person's voice without authorization.

5. **Technical quality**
   - Shorts master: 1080×1920, 9:16, H.264 video, AAC audio, constant 30 fps unless the creative brief requires otherwise.
   - Check duration, audio presence, aspect ratio, playback, spelling, subtitle timing, and visual safe areas before delivery.
   - Never claim a file is uploaded, published, or saved unless the action is confirmed.

## Versioning protocol

- Preserve every delivered master as an immutable version (v1, v2, v3...).
- Maintain a short changelog: what changed, why, and what remains unresolved.
- Do not overwrite a previous master when creating a new iteration.
- A revision must improve a defined dimension: factual rigor, hook clarity, retention, visual accuracy, sound, accessibility, or technical finish.
- Label drafts honestly. A silent or music-only cut is not a narrated final.

## Deep-work protocol

For substantial work, use this sequence:
1. Define the audience promise and success criterion.
2. Identify factual claims and verify them.
3. Challenge the hook, explanation, and likely misunderstandings.
4. Build the storyboard and production assets.
5. Produce the artifact.
6. Run a technical and editorial quality check.
7. Record decisions, limitations, and next experiments.
8. Archive the output and its source materials in the project repository.

Do not add length merely to appear thorough. Prefer deeper verification, better reasoning, and more useful evidence.

## First episode: Müller-Lyer illusion

Core promise: two line shafts can be physically equal while their surrounding arrowheads change how long they appear.

Required proof:
- Display the two shafts with different arrowhead orientations.
- Then remove or neutralize the arrowheads and show identical measured endpoints.
- Name the effect as the Müller-Lyer illusion.
- Explain cautiously that surrounding visual context influences perceived length.

## Release gate

No episode is considered publication-ready until:
- The scientific statement is checked.
- The visual demonstration actually supports the statement.
- Turkish text is proofread.
- Audio and captions are checked.
- The final export is played back and technically inspected.
- The user explicitly approves publication if an external publishing action is available.
