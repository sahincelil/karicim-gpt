# SYSTEM ARCHIVE

A Turkish science channel about visual illusions, perception, the brain, time, and reality.

## Editorial promise
Every video begins with a clear question, gives the viewer a small experiment or striking observation, and ends with a scientifically responsible explanation.

## Content pillars
- 60% visual illusions and perception experiments
- 25% brain mechanisms, memory, attention, and prediction
- 15% time, consciousness, and reality

These are initial test proportions. Rebalance using comparable video cohorts and audience response.

## Publishing experiment
- 3 Shorts per week
- 1 deeper long-form video every two weeks
- 1 Community post per week

Treat this as a four-week experiment, not a permanent rule.

## Measurement framework
1. **Appeal:** impressions, long-form click-through rate, and Shorts viewed vs swiped away / stayed-to-watch where available.
2. **Engagement:** engaged views, average view duration, average percentage viewed, retention, and meaningful comments.
3. **Satisfaction:** returning viewers, subscribers gained, and available satisfaction signals.

Record date ranges and data coverage. Missing analytics are not zero.

## Editorial and security rules
- Distinguish established findings, plausible interpretations, and open questions.
- Cite primary research or reputable scientific sources for factual claims.
- Avoid medical diagnosis, exaggerated neuroscience claims, and misleading brain-hack promises.
- Keep scripts, titles, thumbnails, and uploads as drafts until reviewed by the channel owner.
- Never store passwords, OAuth tokens, recovery codes, API keys, or private analytics exports here.
